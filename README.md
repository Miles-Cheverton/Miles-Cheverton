# The Tall Designer

The Tall Designer is the independent service design consultancy of Miles Cheverton. I lead service design on complex public services. I work with multidisciplinary teams to map how a service works, use research to find where people get stuck, and prototype and test changes before delivery. My work draws on the GDS Service Standard and digital accessibility practice.

## For the user-centred design community

I’ll use GitHub to share practical resources other teams can adapt, from research plans and service mapping templates to examples of how to test an idea.
