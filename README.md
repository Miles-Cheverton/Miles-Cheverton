# The Tall Designer

The Tall Designer is an independent service design and UX consultancy run by Miles Cheverton. I help teams understand where people get stuck in a service, work out what to change and test ideas before committing to a build.

## For the user-centred design community

I’ll use GitHub to share practical resources other teams can adapt, from research plans and service mapping templates to examples of how to test an idea.

## Work with me

I’m available for service design and UX contract and consultancy work. If you have a service you want to improve, [email me](mailto:miles@thetalldesigner.com) or [visit The Tall Designer](https://www.thetalldesigner.com/).
